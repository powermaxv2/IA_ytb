# Gulp privacy policy (Google Play)

Last updated: 23 October 2026

Gulp is a toolbox for Android (photos, videos, sounds, PDFs, messages). **The author collects no personal data.**
The author runs no server for this app: they never receive your files, your messages, your IP address or your
advertising ID.

## What stays on your phone

- Files you give to the tools (photos, videos, sounds, PDFs, exported WhatsApp chats…) are processed **on the phone**, offline.
  Results are saved in the "Gulp" folders of your Gallery, Music or Downloads.
- Your settings, favourites, QR-reader history and timers are stored in the app's private space.
- The Vault encrypts its files in the app's private space; its code never leaves the phone.
- No account, no ads, no analytics, no crash reporting, no tracking tools.
- Android's automatic backup of the app's data is turned off.

## Connections made by the app

| Destination | When | Why | What the service receives |
|---|---|---|---|
| `alphacephei.com` | Only the first time you use "Vocaux en texte" and accept the download | Fetch the French speech-recognition model once (about 42 MB). After that, transcription is offline. | Your IP address, like any download |
| Another app (browser, WhatsApp…) | When you tap "Open", "Send" or "Share" | Open a link or send a result you chose | What you decide to send, to the app you pick |

Gulp sends your data to no other service. The "Lecture à voix haute" (read aloud) tool uses the voice installed on your phone;
depending on your phone, Android's speech engine may be provided by Google.

## Permissions

| Permission | Why | When it is asked |
|---|---|---|
| Camera | Read a QR code, scan a page to PDF | The first time you use one of these tools. Optional. |
| Microphone | Voice recorder | The first time you tap "Record". Optional. |
| Photo location (ACCESS_MEDIA_LOCATION) | The Gomme shows the GPS data hidden in a photo before erasing it | The first time you use the Gomme. Optional. |
| Notifications | Timer and stopwatch (ringing) | First use. Optional. |
| Fingerprint (biometrics) | Unlock the Vault (the code always works) | Optional. |
| Modify system settings | Sonneries: set a cut sound as ringtone, notification or alarm | You allow it yourself in an Android settings page, for this tool only. |
| Internet and network state | One-time download of the speech model (see above) | — |
| Boot completed | Put running timers back after a restart | — |
| Storage (Android 9 and older only) | Save results into public folders | — |

Files are picked with Android's file picker or "Share" menus: Gulp does not read your whole gallery.

## Children

Gulp is not aimed at children in particular and collects no data about anyone.

## Your rights (GDPR)

The author holds no data about you, so there is nothing to access, correct, export or erase. To remove all local data of the app,
uninstall it or use Android Settings › Apps › Gulp › Storage › Clear data.

## Changes

If a future version adds any data processing, this page will be updated before that version is published,
and your consent will be asked where the law requires it.
